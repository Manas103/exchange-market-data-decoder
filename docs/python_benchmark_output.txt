2. Cost of exposing full depth to Python: view versus copy
==============================================================================
Books:                     512 instruments x 2 sides x 16,384 levels = 134.2 MB of level data
Zero-copy sweep:           313.3 us total, 306 ns per view
Materialized sweep:        5,573.9 us total, 5,443 ns per copy
Ratio:                     17.8x  (bytes copied: 0 versus 134.2 MB)

==============================================================================
3. Same comparison for the latency vector (one big buffer)
==============================================================================
Samples:                   2,000,000 uint64 (16.0 MB)
Zero-copy handoff:         300 ns
Materialized copy:         1,811,000 ns
Ratio:                     6,037x

Note: the ratios above are the cost of the handoff, not of the analysis that
follows it. Reducing over a view and reducing over a copy cost the same once
the copy exists. What the binding removes is the allocation and the memcpy on
every single access, which is what makes polling a live book from Python
viable at all.
